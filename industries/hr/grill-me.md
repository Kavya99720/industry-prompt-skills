# HR grill-me

Date: 2026-10-15. Interview: 5 rounds of one or two questions in the main chat, then the owner's changes after the summary. Every answer below was chosen or confirmed by the owner. Nothing here is a design: the brief and skill are not started.

## Answers

| # | Topic | Owner's answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Business types | All four: in-house HR of an employer, staffing/recruitment agency, HR outsourcing/payroll provider, training/background-verification vendor. One skill; the business type decides the scenarios |
| 2 | Callers | Current employees, job candidates, new joiners, managers and ex-employees |
| 3 | Directions (v1) | Inbound support. Outbound: reminders and scheduling only (interview reminders, document follow-ups, onboarding nudges); no offers, rejections or termination news by call. Inbound and outbound are separate Instructions sets |
| 4 | Channel | Voice only in v1, same as all other industries. Chat later |
| 5 | Languages | English always first; the other languages are chosen by the business, same as other industries. Nothing hardcoded |
| 6 | Never does | Disclose another person's data; decide employment outcomes (leave exceptions, hire/reject, promote, terminate, change pay); give legal or labour-law advice; handle complaints itself (it only logs and hands off) |
| 7 | Escalation targets | Harassment, abuse, safety: the HR / ethics / POSH contact. Resignation, termination, legal threats: the HR manager. Payroll errors: the payroll team |
| 8 | Nobody available | Take a callback. Say "Our team will call you back." Never promise a time or window. Harassment and safety are not callback-only: they hand off in the same reply (see 12) |
| 9 | Verification | Employee ID plus one more detail before any personal data. General policy answers need no check. Never read back any stored detail; say only "verified" or "not verified" |
| 10 | Emergency block (always included, word for word like other industries) | Say "Call 1 1 2 right now." and transfer |
| 11 | Self-harm and distress block (always included, word for word) | Includes job loss or workplace distress. Say "Tele-MANAS: 1 4 4 1 6. Emergency: 1 0 8 or 1 1 2." and transfer. Never counsel |
| 12 | Harassment, abuse, POSH block (always included) | Only the business's own POSH / ICC / ethics contact or written route as given. If none is given: say "Our team will confirm that for you." and hand off in the same reply (no callback-only for harassment or safety). Never investigate, never judge, never ask about the accused. Take only name, number and type of concern |
| 13 | Job scam block (staffing/recruitment) | "We never ask for money for a job, interview or offer letter." Never confirm or deny that a call or message was from us. Give the cybercrime helpline 1 9 3 0. If they already paid, add the bank line. Structure copied word for word from banking |
| 14 | Payroll boundary | The agent states published payroll dates and policies only. Never states a person's salary, deductions, tax, PF or bonus amounts. Never takes bank details. Payroll errors are noted and handed off to the payroll team |
| 15 | Staffing rule | Never promise a job, placement, interview result, salary or joining date. Outcomes are decided by the employer |
| 16 | Protected details | Never ask about or note religion, caste, marital status, pregnancy, health, disability, age (beyond "are you 18 or over" where needed) |
| 17 | Data collected | May collect basic contact and request details (name, employee ID, callback number, reason, preferred time). Candidate details only with consent. Never collects ID numbers (Aadhaar, PAN, passport), bank details, or medical and health details |
| 18 | Out of scope | Banking, healthcare and legal matters go to the right party, not the HR agent |
| 19 | Sick leave and health | The agent notes only that the leave is medical. It never asks for or records the illness, diagnosis, medicines or certificate details. If the caller volunteers them, acknowledge only with "I understand." and do not repeat them |
| 20 | Evals | None (DECISIONS 108). Quality: check_variables, check_fixed_lines, manual /prompt-writer tests on test profiles |

Open items: none. Callback wording and the POSH missing-fact line were settled after the summary (rows 8, 12); the sick-leave reading was confirmed (row 19).
